01

The Show vs. Tell Brief

Read first · 3 min

The past / current actions move is where a company describes the real work it is doing for sustainability. The genre rule is simple: show, don't tell.

Telling = making a vague claim. ("We care about communities.") Anyone can say it. The reader has no reason to believe it.

Showing = describing the action in detail — who, where, when, how many, what kind. The reader sees the work and trusts it.

▸ Telling — flat
"We support local communities and care deeply about the environment."
▸ Showing — concrete
"Our team is working with 300 farmers in East Java to plant 50,000 shade-grown coffee trees by the 2026 harvest."

The Five Levers

To turn telling into showing, push at least three of these five levers.

01

Numbers

Exact figures, not vague quantities.

"many" → "1,200"
02

Places

Real locations, not "the region".

"local areas" → "Toraja, Sulawesi"
03

People / Partners

Named groups or roles.

"the community" → "200 smallholder farmers"
04

Time

Specific dates or durations.

"recently" → "since 2022"
05

Methods / Products

Concrete tools or techniques.

"new technology" → "drip irrigation systems"

▸ Red-flag words that signal "telling"

care about support committed to strive for passionate about a lot of / many local communities the environment make a difference work hard help people

These aren't forbidden — but if you use them, you must back them up with the levers above.

02

Spot the Strongest Showing

3 sets · pick the most concrete version

Each set has three versions of the same idea. Choose the one that shows the work most clearly — with numbers, places, partners, or methods.

03

Rewrite With Ingredients

3 rewrites · use the facts provided

Each card has a vague "telling" sentence and a list of ingredients (facts you must include). Rewrite the sentence to show the action — using as many ingredients as you can in a single, natural sentence.

04

Strip the Vague — Add the Proof

3 rewrites · invent realistic detail

Now you provide the detail. Read the vague claim, then write a "showing" version using at least three of the five levers (numbers, places, people, time, methods). You can invent realistic specifics.

05

Past/Current Actions Assignment

Free-write · one full paragraph

Write a past/current actions paragraph that shows the work.

You are writing for Bumi Lestari, a fictional Indonesian apparel brand. The brief below contains a vague claim and a set of real facts. Rewrite it as a 3–5 sentence paragraph for the newsroom page. The goal: every claim should be backed by concrete detail.

▸ The vague version (do NOT copy this)

"At Bumi Lestari, we are passionate about sustainable fashion and committed to making a real difference for local communities and the environment. We work hard every day to support our partners."

▸ The facts (ingredients to use)
  • Partners: 180 weavers in Klungkung, Bali, and Sumba, NTT
  • Materials: natural plant-based dyes (indigo, turmeric, mahogany bark) — no synthetic dyes since 2022
  • Programs: "Tenun Hijau" training programme launched 2021 — has trained 240 weavers so far
  • Numbers: reduced water use by 70% per garment; pay weavers 35% above the regional minimum
  • Action right now: opening a new dye-mixing workshop in Sumba this October
